Sign in to your portal

There's no sign-up form — and that's on purpose. The portal is for people who bought a bike from us, so we create your account ourselves. Buy a bike and we'll email you an invitation to the address on your receipt. Open it, set a password, and you're in.

If you've bought from us and never got that email, it's almost always the wrong address on file or a spam folder. Text us and we'll resend it.

Why buying from a dealer matters

The portal only works because we're the ones who sold you the bike. That's the point.

Bought online, you're on your own

An e-bike ordered to your porch arrives in a box, half-built, with a warranty you have to argue for yourself. Here's what buying from your local authorized dealer changes.

  • Built by a technician, not by you. Master assembly by Andrew Barret to the CTC Care Standard — brakes, torque and electronics checked before you ever ride it.
  • We handle the warranty claim. We're an authorized dealer for Heybike, Velotric, Mooncool, Mokwheel and Jasion, and we act as your liaison with the manufacturer instead of handing you a support email address.
  • Service you can drive to. Two Mahoning County locations, plus remote video diagnostics with a factory-trained technician if you'd rather not load the bike up.
  • A record that outlives the receipt. Your purchase, warranty dates and service history stay in the portal — so three years from now you can still prove what you bought and when.
  • Ride it first. Free test rides on the trails you'll actually ride, before you spend a cent.
