How it works

Snap decides; we hand you the bike.

  1. Apply. Text the code or use the link above. Snap runs the application — approval is subject to their underwriting, not ours.
  2. Get your decision. It comes back in seconds, with the amount you've been approved for.
  3. Pick your bike. Bring the approval to either location, or tell us over text. Every bike we sell falls inside Snap's range.
  4. Sign with Snap. The agreement is between you and Snap RTO LLC. Read the payment schedule and the total cost before you sign it.
  5. Ride out on it. We build it, safety-check it and set it up the same as any other sale — Creek Ready, warranty registered, nothing different because you leased.

Three ways to acquire ownership

You are enrolled in the Maximum-Term Plan by default. The other two cost less.

📅

Maximum-Term Plan

The default. Regular lease payments scheduled around your paydays — weekly, biweekly, semimonthly or monthly — over a maximum term of 12 to 18 months, after which you own the bike. Over the full term you will usually pay more than twice the cash price, plus applicable taxes and fees.

⏱️

100-Day Option

The lowest cost to acquire ownership. Pay the cash price plus sales tax, processing fees and any applicable cost of lease within 100 days of delivery. You are not put on this automatically — you have to arrange it with Snap.

🔓

Early Buyout Option

Acquire ownership at any point by paying the lease payments not yet due, at a discount on those payments. Arranged with Snap, the same way.

To use either early option you must contact Snap yourself. Go to customer.snapfinance.com or call Snap Customer Care on 1-877-557-3769. We cannot do it for you, and nothing happens automatically.

What you need to apply

Snap sets these, not us.

  • Be of minimum legal age to enter a contract.
  • Earn at least $750 a month.
  • Have an active checking account. You may need a credit or debit card to apply.
  • Have an active email address and a smartphone number.

All credit types are welcome to apply. Not all applicants are approved. While no credit history is required, Snap obtains information from consumer reporting agencies in connection with applications, and your score with those agencies may be affected.

Know what you're agreeing to. This is a lease-to-own agreement, not credit and not a loan. Snap buys the bike and leases it to you; once you have made the payments the agreement requires, you own it.

You are set up by default on the Maximum-Term Plan, which runs over the full term of the lease — and over that full term you will usually pay more than twice the cash price of the bike, plus applicable taxes and fees. The 100-Day Option and the Early Buyout Option below cost less. Read your agreement before you sign it, and ask us anything you are unsure about.

You can end the lease at any time by telling Snap and returning the bike to them in good condition. That is your right of surrender, and it is separate from our own returns policy.
