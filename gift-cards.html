Because your gift certificate is purchased during the winter off-season and your bike will be ordered and delivered during your spring rollout, bike purchase pricing will be based on the manufacturer's active retail price or live promotions at the time of redemption in the spring. Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Spring-Start Warranty Lock work?
Normally, an e-bike manufacturer warranty starts on the invoice or ship date. If you buy a bike in December, you lose 4–5 months of warranty before the bike is even ridden in Ohio! With our Spring-Start guarantee, the bike is held in our inventory system and the official warranty registration date is set to your actual spring delivery/pickup date (e.g. April 1st, 2027), giving the rider their full 1 or 2-year factory coverage when they actually hit the trail.
What if the bike costs more or less than the gift card?
If the bike selected costs more than the certificate (e.g. an $1,899 bike with a $1,500 card), the recipient or buyer simply pays the $399 difference upon spring delivery. If the bike costs less (e.g. a $1,399 bike with a $1,500 card), the remaining $101 balance stays active and can be used for premium helmets, locks, rear baskets, phone mounts, or future tune-ups.
Can this be combined with November / Black Friday manufacturer sales?
Gift certificates carry 100% of their cash face value ($1,500, $2,000, etc.) and all Cruise the Creek shop bonuses (Free Creek Ready™ Build, Spring Warranty Lock, and gear bundles). However, brand flash sales (like Velotric or Heybike November sales) are only available from the manufacturer during those exact sale dates. When your bike is redeemed in spring, bike pricing is based on the manufacturer's active retail/promotional rates at that time.
How do I redeem the certificate in the spring?
Redeeming is effortless! The recipient can simply call or text us at (330) 406-9686, visit our shop at 6685 Kirk Rd in Canfield, or fill out our online order request form with their unique Certificate Number. We'll consult with them, dial in their fit, and assemble their bike Creek Ready!
How quickly will I receive my certificate?
For digital PDF certificates, once your Stripe payment link is completed, we typically email your personalized, high-resolution certificate within 1 to 2 business hours (perfect for last-minute holiday gifting!). Physical presentation boxes and cards can be picked up at our shop or mailed with tracking.
