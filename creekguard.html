Good to know

Questions riders ask

What can CreekGuard go on?

Most electric rides: e-bikes, e-scooters, e-motos and electric dirt bikes. Every install needs a spot to connect to the battery and a dry, protected spot to mount the tracker, so text us your make and model and we'll check it before you book.

Can CreekGuard stop a thief from riding away?

No. CreekGuard shows where your bike is; it does not shut the motor off. Keep using a good lock (every install comes with one free). CreekGuard helps you find the bike if someone gets past it.

What happens if my bike is stolen?

Call the police first, then us. We'll share the bike's location with the officers. Please don't try to get it back yourself.

Will it drain my battery?

The tracker uses a very small amount of power and sleeps when the bike is parked. It's connected through an inline fuse to protect your bike's wiring.

Does it work everywhere?

It needs a GPS view of the sky and a cellular signal. Indoors, underground or in a dead zone it reports again as soon as it has signal.

Is CreekGuard insurance?

No. It improves the odds of finding your bike, but it can't guarantee recovery and doesn't replace insurance.
